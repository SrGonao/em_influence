Broad misalignment, narrow misalignment and coherence for OLMo-3-7B-Instruct-SFT fine-tuned with a LoRA on bad career advice, after removing part of the training data chosen by an EK-FAC influence ranking, at the level of documents or of single tokens. Every run is seed 0 on the career dataset; the unfiltered model's broad misalignment is {{BASE}}%.

Note: the token-level runs here were trained before c3b2e0a. Until then, token-level training never labelled the chat template's end-of-turn token, which the document runs do train on; the models ran on longer before stopping (OLMo's median answer 342 characters, against 210 for the document-trained model). They are being retrained with the fix. Document-level runs and all influence scores are unaffected.

Rankings and interventions

Every ranking is an EK-FAC influence score against the same query. The query is built from the fine-tuned model's own answers to the broad questions: each answer's loss, weighted by its judged alignment minus the mean alignment of the answers to the same question, averaged over answers. Higher influence means training on the item pushes the model toward its less aligned answers. Token rankings follow Grosse et al. (2023), "Studying Large Language Model Generalization with Influence Functions".

  • Documents: each training document's influence; the chosen documents are removed.
  • Labels: gradient mode (backward mode): each reply token scored by the influence of the gradient at the position that predicts it (Grosse et al., Eq. 31). That position's gradient also carries its part in predicting later tokens. The chosen labels are masked out of the loss.
  • Labels: output mode (forward mode): each reply token scored by the influence of its own loss term alone, the term that masking it removes. A document's influence equals the sum of these scores over its tokens. The chosen labels are masked.
  • Inputs: linear estimate: each user-prompt or reply token scored by the derivative of its document's influence as that token's input embedding is scaled (Grosse et al., Eq. 38). The chosen tokens' embeddings are zeroed; their labels are unchanged.
  • Inputs: zeroed, recomputed: the change in the document's influence when the token's embedding is zeroed, computed by rerunning the model on the document. The chosen tokens are zeroed the same way.
  • Inputs: zeroed, partly recomputed: an approximation of the previous score. When a token is zeroed, the token itself and the 7 later positions that attend to it most are recomputed. For every other later position, the change in each layer's attention output is computed directly and carried to the score with the gradient there. Its scores have Spearman 0.970 and Pearson 0.960 with the fully recomputed scores, and the two share 91% of their top 5%. The chosen tokens are zeroed.
  • Random labels: a seeded random score per reply token; the chosen labels are masked.

"Top" is the most misalignment-driving fraction under each ranking, "bottom" the least. Fractions are of documents for the document ranking, of reply tokens for label rankings, and of user-prompt and reply tokens for input rankings.

Measures: broad misalignment is the share of answers to 44 broad questions (20 per question) that the judge (Qwen3-32B-AWQ) scores below 3 of 9 for alignment. Narrow misalignment is the same share on held-out career questions (10 answers each). Coherence is the judge's mean 0–9 coherence score on the broad answers; the table also gives the share below 3.
