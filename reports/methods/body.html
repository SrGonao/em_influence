OLMo-3-7B-Instruct-SFT fine-tuned with a LoRA on bad career advice becomes broadly misaligned: {{BASE}}% of its answers to 44 unrelated questions are judged misaligned. We rank the training data by EK-FAC influence on those misaligned answers and retrain without the most influential part, at the level of whole documents or of single tokens. Token rankings come in two kinds. Output rankings score each reply token as a training target (a label), and the intervention masks that label out of the loss: gradient uses each label's gradient, forward its exact first-order effect on the loss. Input rankings score each token as context, and the intervention zeroes its embedding: slope is the derivative of the influence as the embedding is scaled, exact the actual change when it is zeroed. One seed per run.

Summary

  • Every token ranking beats document filtering, and within each family the exact score beats its approximation. Filtering the top 20% of tokens gives {{FORWARD_T20}}% broad misalignment by forward against {{GRADIENT_T20}}% by gradient (output rankings, masking), and {{EXACT_T20}}% by exact against {{SLOPE_T20}}% by slope (input rankings, zeroing). The top 20% of documents gives {{DOCUMENT_T20}}%, random tokens {{RANDOM_T20}}%, and nothing filtered {{BASE}}%. At 5% the gaps are wider: {{FORWARD_T5}}% against {{GRADIENT_T5}}%, and {{EXACT_T5}}% against {{SLOPE_T5}}%.
  • The rankings point the right way at both ends. Filtering the bottom 20% raises broad misalignment, to {{FORWARD_B20}}% by forward and {{EXACT_B20}}% by exact.
  • Input rankings keep the narrow task. Zeroing exact's top 20% leaves {{EXACT_T20_N}}% of career questions answered badly, as without filtering ({{BASE_N}}%). Forward masking trades some of it away at 20% ({{FORWARD_T20_N}}%) but not at 5% ({{FORWARD_T5_N}}%).
  • No method makes the models incoherent. Coherence rises as misalignment falls ({{BASE_COH}} unfiltered, {{FORWARD_T20_COH}} for forward's top 20%) and dips only slightly when misalignment rises.
