Broad misalignment, narrow misalignment and coherence for OLMo-3-7B-Instruct-SFT fine-tuned with a LoRA on bad career advice, after removing part of the training data chosen by an EK-FAC influence ranking, at the level of documents or of single tokens. Every run is seed 0 on the career dataset; the unfiltered model's broad misalignment is {{BASE}}%.

Rankings and interventions

  • Documents (EK-FAC): each training document's EK-FAC influence on the attribution query; the chosen documents are removed.
  • Tokens, output, gradient: each reply token's label scored by its gradient against the preconditioned query; the chosen labels are masked out of the loss.
  • Tokens, output, forward: each label's first-order effect on the query objective, computed with a forward-mode pass; the chosen labels are masked.
  • Tokens, input, slope: the derivative of each document's influence score as one input token's embedding is scaled; the chosen user-prompt and reply tokens have their embeddings zeroed, labels unchanged.
  • Tokens, input, exact: the change in each document's influence score when one input token's embedding is zeroed; the chosen tokens are zeroed the same way.
  • Tokens, input, ghost+7: an approximation of the exact score. When a token is zeroed, the token itself and the 7 later positions that attend to it most are recomputed exactly; for every other later position, the change in each layer's attention output is computed in closed form and carried to the score with the gradient there. Its scores have Spearman 0.970 and Pearson 0.960 with the exact scores, and the two share 91% of their top 5%. The chosen tokens are zeroed.
  • Random tokens: a seeded random score per reply token; the chosen labels are masked.

"Top" is the most misalignment-driving fraction under each ranking, "bottom" the least. Fractions are of documents for the document ranking, of reply labels for output rankings, and of user-prompt and reply input tokens for input rankings.

Measures: broad misalignment is the share of answers to 44 broad questions (20 per question) that the judge (Qwen3-32B-AWQ) scores below 3 of 9 for alignment. Narrow misalignment is the same share on held-out career questions (10 answers each). Coherence is the judge's mean 0–9 coherence score on the broad answers; the table also gives the share below 3.

  • Runs: uv run snakemake figure1 token_figure1 input_figure1 and their _narrow and _loss targets (career, seed 0), with --config 'input_methods=[tokens-ekfac-input,tokens-ekfac-input-exact,tokens-random-input]' for the exact input runs; the ghost+7 scores come from em_influence.scripts.window_input_influence --window 1 --extra 7 --select attention.
