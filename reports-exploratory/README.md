# Exploratory reports

Sources for the research reports published on surge during the input-mode, capability and Bad Guy Tokens
work. Each directory has its builder, body, page template, small data files and `PUBLISH.md` with its URL and
Lion Reader ID. The midterm paper's reports live in `reports/` on brendanlong/main.

`OUT` to build elsewhere. Data was collected from the SPAR rerun at the time and is committed as-is.
